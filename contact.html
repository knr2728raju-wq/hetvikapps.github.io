We build Android apps with intention

Hetvik Apps is an independent studio focused on making everyday mobile experiences feel calm, fast and trustworthy.

Our story

Started by makers who care about the details

Hetvik Apps began with a simple frustration: too many apps are bloated, noisy and careless with your data. We wanted to prove that mobile software can be both delightful and respectful.

So we set out to build a small collection of apps we'd genuinely want on our own phones — starting with ExpenseNest. Every screen is considered, every animation earns its place, and every permission is justified. We're small on purpose, which lets us stay close to the craft and close to the people who use what we make.
