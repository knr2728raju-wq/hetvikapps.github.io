app-ads.txt

How Hetvik Apps authorizes the sellers of our mobile advertising inventory.

What is app-ads.txt?

app-ads.txt (Authorized Sellers for Apps) is an IAB Tech Lab standard that lets app developers publicly declare which companies are authorized to sell their advertising inventory. It's the mobile-app counterpart of ads.txt for websites.

By publishing this file at our website root, we help advertisers verify that ads bought against our apps are legitimate — reducing ad fraud and protecting both advertisers and our users.

How it works

  • Each app's store listing points to our developer website (hetvikapps.com).
  • Ad platforms crawl https://hetvikapps.com/app-ads.txt.
  • They match the entries in the file against the seller trying to sell our inventory.
  • Only authorized sellers listed in the file are trusted.

Our app-ads.txt

Below is the exact content of our live file. You can open the raw file or copy the record.

app-ads.txt 
google.com, pub-9226083958766166, DIRECT, f08c47fec0942fa0
Open raw app-ads.txt

Understanding the record

  • google.com — the domain of the advertising system (the seller).
  • pub-9226083958766166 — our publisher account ID with that ad system.
  • DIRECT — indicates we have a direct relationship with the seller.
  • f08c47fec0942fa0 — the ad system's TAG-ID certification authority ID.
Keep the app-ads.txt file at your domain root so it resolves at https://hetvikapps.com/app-ads.txt. Ad networks may take a few days to crawl and verify it.
