Troubleshooting

Quick fixes for common issues

Most hiccups are solved with one of these steps. Try them in order — if you're still stuck, email us.

Contact support

App won't open or freezes

Force-close the app and reopen it, then make sure you're on the latest version from Google Play.

Data isn't syncing

Check your internet connection and confirm you're signed in. Toggle airplane mode off and on if needed.

Can't sign in

Double-check your email, ensure the app is updated, and try again. Restart the app if the screen doesn't respond.

Something looks off

Clearing the app's cache from Android settings resolves most display glitches without losing your data.
